# Design System & UI/UX Guidelines

## 🎨 Aesthetic Vision
NoteNest delivers a sleek, distraction-free, and modern workspace experience. The design prioritizes visual hierarchy, readability, responsiveness, and fluid micro-interactions.

---

## 🎭 Color Palette & Themes

### Dark Mode (Primary)
- **Background Root**: `#0F1117`
- **Surface Elevation 1**: `#181B24`
- **Surface Elevation 2**: `#222634`
- **Border / Divider**: `#2E3446`
- **Primary Accent**: `#6366F1` (Indigo Glow)
- **Secondary Accent**: `#8B5CF6` (Purple)
- **Text Primary**: `#F3F4F6`
- **Text Secondary**: `#9CA3AF`
- **Text Muted**: `#6B7280`
- **Success**: `#10B981`
- **Warning**: `#F59E0B`
- **Error / Danger**: `#EF4444`

### Light Mode
- **Background Root**: `#F9FAFB`
- **Surface Elevation 1**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Surface Elevation 2**: `#F3F4F6`
- **Border / Divider**: `#E5E7EB`
- **Primary Accent**: `#4F46E5`
- **Text Primary**: `#111827`
- **Text Secondary**: `#4B5563`

---

## 🔤 Typography
- **Primary Font**: `Inter`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `sans-serif`
- **Code & Monospace**: `JetBrains Mono`, `Fira Code`, `Consolas`, `monospace`
- **Reading Font (Optional Serif Mode)**: `Merriweather`, `Georgia`, `serif`

---

## 📐 Layout & Spacing
- **Base Grid**: 4px / 8px scale (`4px`, `8px`, `12px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`)
- **Border Radius**: 
  - Subtle: `6px` (tags, buttons)
  - Medium: `10px` (cards, dialogs)
  - Large: `16px` (modals, floating panels)
- **Shadows**: Soft multi-layered ambient shadows with subtle inner border rings.

---

## ✨ Micro-Interactions & Transitions
- **Hover Transitions**: `150ms cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`
- **Modal Transitions**: `200ms ease-out` enter, `150ms ease-in` exit.
- **Tree Drag & Drop**: Immediate visual feedback with ghost placeholders and highlight lines.
